# Lavoop for Claude

This plugin connects Claude to [Lavoop](https://lavoop.com), a job-matching
service. Once connected, you can ask Claude to search open jobs gathered from
employer career sites, by location, company or job title, and to show a job's
details. Every result links to the job's page on Lavoop, where you apply with
the employer.

## What you need

A Lavoop account. The first time Claude uses Lavoop, you sign in on lavoop.com
and confirm the connection. Claude never sees your password.

## What Claude can see

Job listings and company names: searching all jobs is free on every account.
With a Lavoop subscription, and your permission in Lavoop's Settings, Claude can
also see the jobs matched to you and a gap analysis of how well you fit a job.
It cannot see your messages. You can disconnect at any time in Lavoop's
Settings, under "AI assistants connected".
