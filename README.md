# Lavoop for Claude

This plugin connects Claude to [Lavoop](https://lavoop.com), a job-matching
service. Once connected, you can ask Claude to search open jobs gathered from
employer career sites, by location, company or job title, and to show a job's
details. Every result includes the employer's own link, so you apply directly
with the employer.

## What you need

A Lavoop account. The first time Claude uses Lavoop, you sign in on lavoop.com
and confirm the connection. Claude never sees your password.

## What Claude can see

Job listings and company names. It cannot see your CV, profile, matches or
messages unless your Lavoop account includes those features and you have
switched them on. You can disconnect at any time in Lavoop's Settings, under
"AI assistants connected".
